About the project

The idea

Most chat platforms optimize for engagement. Fellowship Chat optimizes for belonging — rooms where people actually know each other, governed openly, with the community's interests built into the product.

Who it's for

The AGIF fellowship community, its ventures and orders — and any community that wants a home designed for depth over noise. Waitlist members get founding-member access.

What's coming

The first three pillars of the platform.

💬

Rooms

Spaces with purpose — anchored to communities, projects, and gatherings, not an endless scroll.

🛡️

Moderation

Governance-grade moderation — the AGIF approach to stewardship, applied to conversation.

✨

Identity

Profiles with continuity and reputation — who you are travels with you across the fellowship.
